## Overview

A funnel page designed for cold paid traffic, where every section moves the visitor toward one decision.

## The Challenge

Cold traffic rarely converts on a standard product page — it needs proof, urgency and a simple pricing choice.

## Strategy

Hero promise, benefits, bundle pricing with a highlighted best value, then reviews and FAQ to close objections.

## Design Approach

High-contrast layout with bold typography and a clearly recommended bundle.

## Development

Fast standalone landing page with a sticky add-to-cart bar and one-click bundle switching.

## Optimization

Offer, headline and pricing tested in rotation with full event tracking back to the ad platforms.

## Key Features

- Bundle pricing with best-value anchor
- Countdown-backed offer
- Verified review strip
- Sticky add-to-cart bar

## Outcomes

- Higher conversion rate from paid traffic
- Larger average order through bundles
- Clean attribution for ad spend
