## Overview

Designed and developed a modern, high-converting e-commerce website that enables businesses to showcase products, manage online sales, and provide customers with a seamless shopping experience. The website was built with a clean and intuitive interface, focusing on user experience, performance, and sales optimization.

The platform allows customers to browse products, search and filter items, add products to their cart, and complete purchases through a secure checkout process. The website was fully optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices to ensure a consistent shopping experience across all platforms.
