## Overview

A demo build that shows how a tech brand can sell multiple SKUs without losing focus on the flagship product.

## The Challenge

Gadget stores often list everything at once, so shoppers never learn what the brand is actually known for.

## Strategy

Give the flagship product the hero, back it with proof and benefits, then present the rest of the range in a uniform grid.

## Design Approach

Deep navy surfaces with gold accents, product shots on a consistent backdrop and high-contrast pricing.

## Development

Reusable benefit, review and product-grid sections plus a sticky add-to-cart on mobile.

## Optimization

Ad landing traffic routed to a matching offer page, with events tracked end to end for ROAS reporting.

## Key Features

- Flagship product hero
- Benefits and guarantee row
- Uniform product grid with ratings
- Sticky mobile add to cart

## Outcomes

- Clearer brand story on first scroll
- Higher add-to-cart rate from paid traffic
- Reliable conversion tracking
