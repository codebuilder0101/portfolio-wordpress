## Overview

Vital Labs shows how supplement brands can sell a routine instead of a single bottle.

## The Challenge

One-off supplement purchases rarely repeat without education and a reason to subscribe.

## Strategy

Bundle products into routines, explain ingredients plainly, and make subscription the default choice.

## Design Approach

Clinical but warm palette, clear ingredient tables and consistent packaging photography.

## Development

Subscription options, routine bundles and an ingredient glossary built as reusable sections.

## Optimization

SEO content mapped to ingredient and benefit searches, with flows that nudge refills before a bottle runs out.

## Key Features

- Routine bundles
- Subscribe-and-save defaults
- Ingredient education pages
- Refill reminder flows

## Outcomes

- More subscription sign-ups
- Growing organic traffic from ingredient searches
- Higher repeat purchase rate
