## Overview

A concept store for a small handmade jewelry catalogue where each piece needs space and detail.

## The Challenge

A small catalogue can look thin on a standard e-commerce grid, and handmade detail is lost in small thumbnails.

## Strategy

Use a slower, larger layout with fewer products per row and story content between collections.

## Design Approach

An elegant minimal layout with large imagery, restrained type and a warm accent used sparingly.

## Development

Built lightweight templates with lazy-loaded galleries so large imagery does not slow the store down.

## Optimization

Image sizes and formats were tuned for mobile, and each product page received unique descriptive metadata.

## Key Features

- Large-format product galleries
- Made-to-order and lead-time messaging
- Story sections between collections
- Lightweight, lazy-loaded imagery

## Outcomes

- Stronger product presentation for a small catalogue
- Improved visual consistency across templates
- Better mobile image performance
