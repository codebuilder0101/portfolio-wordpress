## Overview

Wurus Biosciences is a pioneering bioscience lab focused on culturomics and microbiome services, building a global vault of live, culturable microbes for applications in human health and agriculture.

I was responsible for delivering a highly professional and credible corporate website design that clearly communicates their complex, proprietary technology and target audiences — academic, pharma, and biotech partners. This work successfully provided a scientific platform to accelerate discovery and development.
