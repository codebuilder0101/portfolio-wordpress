## Overview

A demo store showing how a small skincare range can be merchandised around customer intent instead of product categories.

## The Challenge

Customers shop skincare by concern and routine. A catalogue split only by product type makes them do the work of building a routine themselves.

## Strategy

Build collections around concerns and routines, and add bundle pages that group products into a complete regimen.

## Design Approach

Soft neutral tones, plenty of breathing room, and ingredient-led content blocks that stay readable on small screens.

## Development

Collection templates were extended with intro content and educational blocks; bundles were built as products with linked components.

## Optimization

On-page SEO foundations were set across collections and products, with descriptive headings and unique metadata.

## Key Features

- Concern-based and routine-based collections
- Bundle pages for complete routines
- Ingredient and usage content blocks
- SEO-ready collection templates

## Outcomes

- Cleaner navigation aligned to how customers shop
- Stronger product presentation with supporting content
- Improved on-page SEO structure
