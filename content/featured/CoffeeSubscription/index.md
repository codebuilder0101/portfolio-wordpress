## Overview

A demo storefront for a specialty coffee roaster offering both single bags and recurring deliveries.

## The Challenge

Subscription and one-time purchase options often compete on the same product page and leave customers unsure of what they are buying.

## Strategy

Present a clear two-option selector with plain-language differences, and explain delivery frequency and cancellation up front.

## Design Approach

Warm, product-led design with a simple selector pattern that reads clearly on a phone.

## Development

Subscription app integration with a customised product form and a dedicated plan-management entry point in the account area.

## Optimization

Purchase options, delivery timing and cancellation terms were placed together to reduce hesitation before checkout.

## Key Features

- One-time and subscription purchase selector
- Plain-language plan terms on the product page
- Account entry point for managing deliveries
- Roast and flavour filtering

## Outcomes

- Clearer purchase options on product pages
- Stronger presentation of subscription terms
- Simpler mobile buying flow
