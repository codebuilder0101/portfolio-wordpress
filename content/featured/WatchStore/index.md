## Overview

A concept store for a men's watch and accessories brand where the product page carries most of the buying decision.

## The Challenge

Considered purchases need more information than a single image and a price. The challenge was presenting specifications, materials and warranty details without overwhelming the page.

## Strategy

Treat the product page as the homepage. Lead with a gallery, follow with a short specification table, then answer common objections directly on the page.

## Design Approach

A dark, restrained interface that lets metal and leather product photography stand out, with a single accent colour reserved for actions.

## Development

Theme customization added a specification block, a sticky add-to-cart bar on mobile, and a related-products section driven by collection metafields.

## Optimization

Mobile received a sticky purchase bar, compressed imagery, and a shortened checkout path from product page to cart.

## Key Features

- Sticky mobile add-to-cart bar
- Specification and materials block
- Gallery with zoom and detail shots
- Objection-handling FAQ on the product page

## Outcomes

- Better mobile experience on product pages
- Clearer product presentation and specification detail
- More consistent calls to action across templates
