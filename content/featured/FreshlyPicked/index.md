## Overview

A demo build showing how a seasonal offer can headline a storefront without hiding the rest of the catalogue.

## The Challenge

Promotions often take over a homepage and push evergreen bestsellers out of view, which hurts full-price sales after the campaign ends.

## Strategy

Give the campaign a dedicated hero and banner, then immediately return to bestsellers and category browsing below it.

## Design Approach

Warm, playful palette with large product photography and clear discount badging that stays on-brand.

## Development

Scheduled campaign sections, collection filters and a colour and size selector tuned for mobile thumbs.

## Optimization

Email capture wired to a welcome flow, with abandoned cart and post-purchase automations supporting the promotion.

## Key Features

- Scheduled campaign hero and promo banner
- Bestseller row directly under the offer
- Mobile-first size and colour selection
- Email capture connected to automated flows

## Outcomes

- Campaign visibility without burying evergreen products
- More email subscribers captured during the promotion
- Faster browsing on mobile devices
