## Overview

Lumiren shows how a customized Shopify theme can make a small catalogue feel like a premium retail brand.

## The Challenge

The original store used a default theme with mismatched imagery, so a premium-priced product looked like a generic dropshipping shop.

## Strategy

Lead with one editorial hero, standardise every product image, and let whitespace and typography carry the brand.

## Design Approach

Warm neutral palette, serif wordmark, consistent 1:1 product cards and a calm trust bar instead of loud badges.

## Development

Custom sections for the hero, bestseller carousel and trust row, all editable from the theme customizer.

## Optimization

Bestsellers surfaced above the fold, shipping threshold announced sitewide, and welcome plus abandoned-cart flows wired to Klaviyo.

## Key Features

- Editorial hero with a single call to action
- Uniform bestseller carousel
- Trust and shipping bar
- Automated email flows

## Outcomes

- Premium presentation that matches the price point
- Faster path from homepage to product page
- More repeat purchases from email flows
