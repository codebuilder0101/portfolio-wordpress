## Overview

A full redesign of a baby products dropshipping store, moving from a cluttered default theme to a clean, high-end storefront led by the new Zurich collection.

## The Challenge

The original store buried its best-selling collection under generic sections and stock imagery, so first-time visitors had no clear reason to keep scrolling.

## Strategy

Lead with one collection, one promise and one call to action, then support it with social proof and simple category entry points.

## Design Approach

Soft neutral palette, generous whitespace and lifestyle photography sized consistently across every card.

## Development

Custom Shopify sections for the hero, collection strip and trust bar so the team can swap seasonal offers without a developer.

## Optimization

Product pages restructured around the buying decision, with faster image delivery and a shortened mobile checkout path.

## Key Features

- Single-offer hero with collection-led navigation
- Reusable seasonal promo sections
- Trust and shipping bar above the fold
- Optimized mobile product galleries

## Outcomes

- Clearer first-scroll message for new visitors
- Higher add-to-cart rate from collection pages
- Consistent premium presentation across templates
