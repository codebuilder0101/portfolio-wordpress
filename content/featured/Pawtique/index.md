## Overview

Pawtique demonstrates how social proof and simple category entry points can carry a mid-sized catalogue.

## The Challenge

Pet catalogues span many species, sizes and price points, which makes browsing confusing without strong filtering.

## Strategy

Split navigation by pet first, then by need, and lead every collection card with a rating.

## Design Approach

Soft green palette, warm lifestyle photography and rounded, readable product cards.

## Development

Filterable collections, size and colour variants, and reusable review sections.

## Optimization

Free-shipping threshold surfaced in the announcement bar and cart to lift average order value.

## Key Features

- Pet-first navigation
- Ratings on every product card
- Filterable collections
- Shipping threshold prompts

## Outcomes

- Easier catalogue browsing
- Stronger trust signals above the fold
- Higher average order value
