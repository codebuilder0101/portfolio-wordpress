## Overview

Nordil is a concept storefront for a home and living brand selling ceramics, textiles and small furniture. The goal was a storefront that feels considered and makes a wide catalogue easy to browse.

## The Challenge

Home and living catalogues mix many product types and price points. Without clear collection logic, customers struggle to find what they came for and leave before reaching a product page.

## Strategy

Group the catalogue by room and by material, keep the main menu short, and let filtering handle depth. Product pages lead with imagery and practical details such as dimensions and materials.

## Design Approach

A neutral palette, generous whitespace and a consistent image ratio across collection cards. Typography carries the hierarchy so the products stay the loudest element on the page.

## Development

Built with reusable Shopify sections so the team can rearrange the homepage, add editorial blocks and update collections without touching code.

## Optimization

Product pages were restructured around the buying decision: imagery, key specs, delivery information, then supporting content. Mobile navigation was simplified to a two-level menu.

## Key Features

- Room-based and material-based collection structure
- Reusable homepage sections for editorial content
- Filterable collection pages
- Product pages with specification and delivery blocks

## Outcomes

- Improved visual consistency across collection and product pages
- Cleaner navigation with fewer menu levels
- Stronger product presentation on mobile
